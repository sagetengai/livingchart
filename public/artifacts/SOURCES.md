# Chart preview data

Bathymetry and elevation are derived from the GEBCO Bathymetric Compilation
Group (2026), GEBCO_2026 Grid, DOI [10.5285/4f68d5c7-45eb-f999-e063-7086abc036fa](https://doi.org/10.5285/4f68d5c7-45eb-f999-e063-7086abc036fa).
See the [GEBCO terms of use](https://www.gebco.net/data-products/gridded-bathymetry-data).
The preview is for visualization and unsuitable for navigation.

The land mask and clipped coastline are derived from GSHHG 2.3.7 by Paul Wessel
and Walter H. F. Smith. The shoreline data is distributed under LGPL 3.0 or
later. See the [upstream GSHHG source and shapefiles](https://www.soest.hawaii.edu/wessel/gshhg/).
The chart generators are in `experiments/phase-1-geography` and `tools/cartography`
in the [Living Chart repository](https://github.com/sagetengai/livingchart).
